## Understanding Global Carbon Systems

### The Problem

**Every company has a carbon footprint.**

From manufacturing to shipping, companies emit CO₂. Governments worldwide are setting **emission limits** (carbon caps) for industries.

### The Solution: Carbon Markets

Governments have created **Carbon Trading Systems** to incentivize emission reductions:

---

## 🔄 How Carbon Markets Work

### 1. Carbon Credit System

**Concept:** Each ton of CO₂ = 1 Carbon Credit

**How it works:**
```
Company A allows ↓ emissions
  ↓
Earns carbon credits
  ↓
Sells credits to Company B
  ↓
Company B (high emissions) buys credits
  ↓
Incentives all companies to reduce emissions
```

**Example:**
- Tesla: Low emissions → Sells credits → Makes $$$ 💰
- Gas car manufacturer: High emissions → Buys credits → Pays penalty

### 2. Emission Trading Scheme (ETS)

**Real-world example: EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS)**

```
Government sets total emission cap for industry
  ↓
Companies get emission allowances
  ↓
Companies can trade allowances with each other
  ↓
If you exceed cap → Fine or buy credits
  ↓
If you're below cap → Sell credits for profit
```

**Countries with ETS:**
- 🇪🇺 EU (Strongest ETS)
- 🇨🇳 China (Largest by volume)
- 🇬🇧 UK
- 🇨🇦 Canada
- 🇯🇵 Japan
- 🇮🇳 India (Developing carbon market)
